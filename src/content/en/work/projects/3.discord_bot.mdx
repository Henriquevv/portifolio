## Overview

**Payment Management Bot** was developed to automate the organization of shared subscriptions within Discord servers.  
The system replaces manual tracking performed through individual messages with a complete flow: automatic calculation of amounts due, payment records, monthly history, receipt delivery, Google Drive synchronization, and pending-payment alerts.

The project arose from the need to manage a subscription divided between several members, ensuring transparency and reducing human errors in the billing process.

---

## Architecture and Stack

The solution was designed to run continuously and reliably, with cloud-based data persistence:

- **Language:** Python 3.13  
- **Discord Bot:** Official Library `discord.py`  
- **Data Storage:** Google Drive (automatic synchronization of records and receipts)  
- **Local Persistence:** State files and system logs  
- **Automation:** Internal schedules for daily notifications and dollar exchange rate updates  
- **Infrastructure:** Continuous process running in a Linux environment using system services  

The main focus of the architecture was to ensure reliability, fault tolerance and traceability of payments.

---

## Main Features

### 1. Automatic Subscription Calculation  
The bot updates the dollar rate, calculates the total subscription value and automatically divides it among registered members.  
Displays the individual value, the total and the due date in a single command.

### 2. Payment and Receipt Record  
Users can register that they have made the payment, including sending proof.  
The bot automatically synchronizes files with Google Drive, keeping the payment history organized.

### 3. Monthly History and Current Status  
The system maintains an individual history per month for each member, allowing quick queries on late or completed payments.

### 4. Member Management  
Administrators can add, remove, link accounts, and set up automatic payment for specific users.

### 5. Automatic Notifications  
The bot sends periodic messages with payment reminders, overdue alerts and registration confirmations.  
This way, all members remain aligned throughout the month.

---

## Challenges and Solutions

The biggest challenge was ensuring that the system was simple for users, but internally robust.  
Some critical points:

### File Sync on Google Drive  
Recording vouchers and ensuring consistency between local data and the shared folder required fault-tolerant synchronization logic.  
The solution included integrity validation, timestamps and logs for auditing.

### Status and History Control  
To avoid inconsistencies, it was necessary to create a clear structure that separated current month data, historical data and individual user settings.

### Notification Automation  
Notifications needed to occur daily and without depending on third parties.  
The solution was to implement an asynchronous loop with error tolerance and last execution recording.

## Impact and Results

The bot brought organization and transparency to the monthly cost division process:

- Significant reduction in manual errors when confirming payments  
- Centralized and trackable history of every month  
- Processes that previously depended on informal arrangements are now automated  
- Greater confidence and predictability in subscription management
